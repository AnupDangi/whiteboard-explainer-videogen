# Binary search

[S1] Binary search needs a sorted array; it inspects the middle element. [S2] If target is smaller, the right half is impossible and discarded, and vice versa. [S3] Repeating on the remaining half shrinks candidates exponentially: O(log n) steps. [S4] Example: in [1,4,8,12,19,27,35], target 27 compares 12, then 27 found after eliminating halves.
