# Photosynthesis as matter and energy transformation

[S1] Inputs are carbon dioxide and water; outputs are sugar and oxygen: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. [S2] Light supplies energy captured by chlorophyll; it is not matter incorporated into sugar. [S3] Carbon and water atoms become sugar and oxygen atoms; energy is converted to chemical form. [S4] Oxygen released comes from water splitting, and sugar stores the captured energy.
