# Self-attention as weighted information mixing

[S1] Each position needs to gather relevant information from other positions. [S2] Relevance scores compare a seeker (query) against candidates (keys); weights normalize to sum 1. [S3] The output mixes contents (values) by those weights: high relevance contributes more. [S4] Q/K/V names arrive only here: Q seeks, K matches, V supplies content.
