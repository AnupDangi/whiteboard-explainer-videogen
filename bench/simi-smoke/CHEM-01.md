# Ionic versus covalent bonding

[S1] Ionic bonding transfers electrons, forming oppositely charged ions that attract (e.g. NaCl). [S2] Covalent bonding shares electron pairs between nuclei (e.g. H2, one shared pair). [S3] Transfer yields charged ions and typically brittle crystals; sharing yields defined molecules. [S4] Electronegativity difference predicts character: large favors ionic, small favors covalent.
