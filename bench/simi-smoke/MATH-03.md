# Derivative as instantaneous rate of change

[S1] Average rate of change over an interval is rise over run: (f(x+h)-f(x))/h. [S2] As the interval h shrinks toward zero, the secant line approaches the tangent line at the point. [S3] The limit is the instantaneous rate of change, the derivative f'(x), equal to the tangent slope. [S4] It predicts value just after x but is defined exactly at x via the limit.
