# MATH-02

In a right triangle the sides meeting at the right angle are legs a and b; the side opposite is hypotenuse c. Squares built on each side have areas a-squared, b-squared, c-squared. For any right triangle, the two smaller square areas add exactly to the largest: a2 + b2 = c2. Example: legs 3 and 4 give 9 + 16 = 25, so c = 5.
