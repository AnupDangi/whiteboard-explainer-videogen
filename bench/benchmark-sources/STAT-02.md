# STAT-02

Conditional probability restricts to cases where B happened: P(A|B) = count(A and B)/count(B). Bayes flips the condition using prior, likelihood, and normalizer: P(D|+) = P(+|D)P(D)/P(+). Example: 1% disease, 90% true-positive, 9% false-positive gives about 9% posterior after one positive. Base rate dominates: rare conditions stay unlikely after one imperfect test.
