# Mitosis

Mitosis is the division of a eukaryotic cell nucleus that produces two genetically identical daughter nuclei. It follows interphase, when the cell copies its DNA so each chromosome consists of two identical sister chromatids joined at a centromere.

In prophase the chromosomes condense and the spindle begins to form. In prometaphase the nuclear envelope breaks down and spindle fibres attach to the chromosomes. In metaphase the chromosomes line up along the cell's equator. In anaphase the sister chromatids separate and are pulled to opposite poles. In telophase nuclear envelopes re-form around each set of chromosomes.

Cytokinesis then divides the cytoplasm, producing two cells. In animal cells a ring of protein pinches the cell in two. In plant cells a new cell wall forms between the nuclei. A human cell with 46 chromosomes produces two cells that each have 46.
