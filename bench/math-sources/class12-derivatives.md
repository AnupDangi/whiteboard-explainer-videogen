# The derivative

The derivative measures how fast a function changes. For a function f of x, the derivative f prime of x is the limit of the average rate of change, f of x plus h minus f of x all divided by h, as h goes to zero.

Geometrically, the derivative is the slope of the tangent line to the curve at x.

The power rule: the derivative of x to the power n is n times x to the power n minus 1. So the derivative of x squared is 2 x, and the derivative of 3 x is 3. The derivative of a sum is the sum of the derivatives.

At a maximum or a minimum of a smooth curve, the derivative is zero.
